Xưởng video – Privacy Policy

Xưởng video runs on the owner's own computer. It stores access tokens for TikTok and Facebook only on that computer (and in the owner's private Google Drive app folder for syncing between the owner's two computers), and uses them only to upload the channel's own videos and read their view and comment counts.

It collects no personal data from other people, shows no ads, and shares nothing with third parties.

To delete all data: disconnect the account in the app settings, or contact chungtrantuantu@gmail.com.
